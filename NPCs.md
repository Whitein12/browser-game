
# NPCs:

## Cinderholt:

### The merchant:

#### Personality:

A young and ambitious shopkeeper. Friends with the player since their childhood: they both grew up in the town of Cinderholt.
He likes people and being around them: he spends his profit quite frequently in the tavern.

#### Quotes:

"The Ember Forge is quite stirring nowadays. A few days ago a wanderer came into town to buy supplies: he swore dark clouds were gathering above the mountain peaks"

"The apothecar used to say that alcohol can make wonders. She's clearly wise: I can't agree more with her"

"Did you know that the Witch's minions all have names? Larry, Perry, Terry, Natty, Matty and Sir Roderick the Third. I can't imagine what happened to the first two."

### The smith:

#### Personality:

And old and seemingly cranky craftsman. Don't let the cover fool you: he just enthusiastic about his work as in his youth.

#### Quotes:

"When I was just a child, my father used to tell me stories about the forgemaster Frynnar. The tales said they lived under the fiery mountains of Ember Forge and used the molten magma of the depths to forge wondrous weapons and tools. Sometimes when I wander in the clearings I gaze towards those mountains and wish the fairytales are true."

"I've offered the Druid many times to craft some help for their companions. They just laughed and said Mother Nature took care of everything already. Hah! I'm ready for a challenge then"

"My biggest work yet? Mhmmm... you met the Paladin? Her mace was a huge hassle, almost sixty pounds of hearty steel. I wonder how can she even lift that monster"

"The Kensei knows a lot about forging, but she has a lot of rules on how to work with her blade. I don't mind helping her out, but man I don't like a backseat forger."

"I've been forging every kind of metal, but the spellweaver can surprise me everytime with his requests. Last time he asked me to grow a plant for his next wand while he was away on an adventure: couldn't believe my own eyes when the plant grew to be a metalic tree"

"Some people get the creeps from the Witch when she's around. Personally I love when she visits: she orders a unique weapon for every one of her minions. They'll always so fun to make"

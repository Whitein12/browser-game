# Return to cinderholt:

After the end of the Dungeon: Bandit Bastion the player returns to Cinderholt. The fires have been stopped, but the raid made quite the damage on the town nonetheless. There are houses entirely destroyed, while some survided but paid a heavy toll. 

As the player comes back the merchant welcomes them back. He explains to the player that the bandits did a suprise raid: caught the local guards off guard, killed them and then pillaged the town. 

The player says that they will rebuild the town. 

# Rebuild Cinderholt:

The costs are hefty: lots of gold and resources -> but if the player was thorough they could find a lot of resources in the first two dungeons which helps a lot. 

The four main buildings to repair: 

- the tavern/town hall
- the apothecary
- the merchant's shop
- the smith's forge
